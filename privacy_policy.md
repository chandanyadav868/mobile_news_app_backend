## 1. Introduction
Welcome to **News Flow**, a mobile news application developed by **TwoGamers** ("we", "our", or "us"). We value your privacy and are committed to protecting any information you share with us.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how it is used, and the choices you have regarding your data when using the News Flow mobile application.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### A. Information You Provide
- **Account Details (Optional):** You can freely read news as a guest. If you choose to register an account, we collect your name and email address to manage your account and synchronize your saved stories across devices.
- **Support & Feedback:** If you reach out to us with questions or feedback, we collect your email address and message contents to assist you.

### B. Information Collected Automatically
- **Reading Preferences:** Your saved bookmarks, preferred news categories, and reading history stored to personalize your experience.
- **Device & Usage Information:** Basic device details (such as device model, operating system version, and general network state) and non-identifying crash logs to ensure app performance and fix technical issues.
- **Push Notification Token:** If you enable notifications, a device token is generated so we can deliver breaking news updates and headlines. You can disable notifications at any time in the app or your device settings.

---

## 3. Third-Party Services & Advertising

We partner with trusted third-party service providers to help operate and support the app:

- **Google AdMob (Advertising):** We display advertisements provided by Google AdMob to keep News Flow free for readers. Google AdMob may use anonymized device advertising identifiers to deliver relevant ads and prevent fraud. You can opt out of personalized advertising at any time through your device settings:
  - *Android:* Settings > Google > Ads > Opt out of Ads Personalization (or Delete Advertising ID).
  - *iOS:* Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  - Learn more at [Google's Privacy & Terms](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).
- **Notification & Crash Diagnostics:** We use industry-standard cloud and diagnostics tools to deliver push notifications and monitor app stability without collecting personal identity details.

We **do not sell, rent, or trade** your personal information to third parties.

---

## 4. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect strictly to:
- Deliver news articles, summaries, and audio reading features.
- Save your bookmarked stories and topic preferences.
- Send breaking news alerts (only if you have enabled notifications).
- Ensure smooth app performance, diagnose bugs, and maintain security.
- Display non-intrusive advertisements.

---

## 5. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical safeguards and industry-standard encryption (including HTTPS/TLS) to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration. Sensitive session data on your device is stored using secure platform storage.

---

## 6. User Rights & Account Deletion

You have complete control over your personal data:

- **In-App Account Deletion:** You can permanently delete your account and all associated personal data directly within the app by going to:  
  **Profile / Settings > Delete Account**.  
  Upon confirmation, your profile, credentials, and saved bookmarks are deleted immediately.
- **Email Request:** You may also request deletion of your account and personal data by sending an email to **support@twogamers.com** with the subject `"Delete My Account"`. We will process your request promptly.

---

## 7. Children's Privacy

News Flow is intended for general audiences and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that a child under 13 has provided personal data, we will take immediate steps to delete such information.

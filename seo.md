## 💡 6. Tips to Rank Higher on Google Play (ASO Checklist)

- **Use Screenshots with Bold Captions:** The first 3 screenshots drive 80% of downloads. Use text overlays like:
  - *Screen 1:* "Stay Informed in 60 Seconds"
  - *Screen 2:* "Listen to News Hands-Free"
  - *Screen 3:* "Breaking News as it Happens"
  - *Screen 4:* "Personalized Categories Just for You"
- **Do Not Keyword-Stuff:** Keep keyword density between 2% and 4%. Natural, readable sentences convert much better than repeated keyword lists.
- **Encourage Initial Ratings:** Ask happy users to rate the app after reading 5+ articles. Higher early ratings boost Play Store algorithm discovery.
